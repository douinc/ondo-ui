**v0.2.1**

Semantic colors beyond `destructive`. The `info`, `success`, and `warning`
tokens now surface as first-class variants across the registry:

- [`Button`](/docs/components/button), [`Badge`](/docs/components/badge), and
  [`Alert`](/docs/components/alert) gain `info`, `success`, and `warning`
  variants — the same soft-tint recipe as `destructive`.
- [`Alert`](/docs/components/alert) variants now fill their background and border
  with the semantic tint, plus a new `primary` variant.
- [`Toast`](/docs/components/toast) messages
  (`toast.success` / `.info` / `.warning` / `.error`) are themed with the
  semantic tokens.
