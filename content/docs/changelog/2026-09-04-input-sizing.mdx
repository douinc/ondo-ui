---
title: Input padding and radius
description: Input and Textarea gain wider padding and rounder corners, and two dead radius expressions are removed.
version: 1.8.2
---

**v1.8.2**

- Inner horizontal padding widens two steps on `Input`: `12 / 14 / 14 / 14 / 16 / 20px` for `xs` through `2xl`. Text no longer crowds the border.
- Corner rounding moves up one step: `10px` for `xs` through `lg`, `14px` for `xl`, `18px` for `2xl`.
- `Textarea` follows the same padding and rounding so a form mixing the two stays aligned. Its minimum heights are unchanged.
- Dropped `rounded-[min(var(--radius-md),8px)]` and `rounded-[min(var(--radius-md),10px)]` from the `xs` and `sm` sizes. Both collapsed to the base radius, so the expression never did anything.
- `Input` and `Textarea` now carry a `data-size` attribute, so a consumer can target one size without repeating the variant list.
- Dropped the `shadow-xs` drop shadow from `Input`, `Textarea`, `InputGroup`, `NativeSelect`, `Select` and `Checkbox`. The border alone carries the field, and the shadow made controls float above the surface they sit on.
